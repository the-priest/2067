# 2067: Hornfall 0.1.0 beta

The first playable build of the hunt. Download the zip for your system, unzip, and run
`2067.exe` (Windows) or `2067.x86_64` (Linux). Saves go in your user folder.

## What's in it

- A 2 km valley after the Fall: farmland, dead forest, badlands, ridges,
  a lake and marsh, and the mothership broken across the crash basin.
- Seven alien-animal hybrids, around 80 animals about at any time. Every
  animal's body, size and horns are its own; horns come in different styles,
  curls, colours and sizes, glowing beads, and the odd mutant with two pairs.
- Every heart is in a different place. Heart, lung, gut, spine and brain
  shots all behave differently; wounded animals leave glowing blood trails.
- Real bullet drop, iron sights, scopes with breath holding, binocular
  spotting that marks animals for you.
- Harvest hides and horns, sell them to Mae over the radio or mount them on
  your farmhouse wall. Contracts, ranks, and six guns from Grandpa's .30-30
  to a Visitor rail gun, plus gear like heart scanners, thermal, cloak and a
  recon drone.
- Graphics presets (Low to Ultra) in Settings. Laptops: start on Low.
