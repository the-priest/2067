# 2067: Hornfall 0.3.0 beta

The first playable build of the hunt. Download the zip for your system, unzip, and run
`2067.exe` (Windows) or `2067.x86_64` (Linux). Saves go in your user folder.

## New in 0.3

- Every rifle has a scope now, Grandpa's .30-30 included (3x).
- Full controller support, laid out like the big hunting games: sticks to
  move and look (with a little aim friction over animals), LT aim, RT fire,
  LB hold breath, RB next gun, D-pad for binoculars, light, caller and scent,
  D-pad up/down to zoom on the scope, B to crouch, X to use or hold to
  harvest, Y reload, R3 thermal, LB+B cloak, LB+A drone. Rumble on shots and
  hits. Every menu works with the pad.
- Six new mutated species: Tigrath (tiger), Ursagore (bear with ram horns),
  Mammothar (elephant), Rhinox (rhino with three horns), Girafflux (giraffe
  with a crystal crown) and Leonix (lion with a mane of crystal quills).
  Thirteen hybrids in all.
- Six hunting camps hidden around the valley. Find one and you can fast
  travel there (and home to the farm) from the map.

## New in 0.2

- Bullet cam: long shots into the heart, lungs, brain or spine play back in
  slow motion, riding the round into the animal and showing the hit through
  its hide (turn it off in Settings).
- The mothership is far bigger and breaks the skyline over the crash basin,
  which now sits out in the open east of the ridges.
- Fixed: carcasses can be harvested (hold E over the body).

## What's in it

- A 2 km valley after the Fall: farmland, dead forest, badlands, ridges,
  a lake and marsh, and the mothership broken across the crash basin.
- Seven alien-animal hybrids, around 80 animals about at any time. Every
  animal's body, size and horns are its own; horns come in different styles,
  curls, colours and sizes, glowing beads, and the odd mutant with two pairs.
- Every heart is in a different place. Heart, lung, gut, spine and brain
  shots all behave differently; wounded animals leave glowing blood trails.
- Real bullet drop, iron sights, scopes with breath holding, binocular
  spotting that marks animals for you.
- Harvest hides and horns, sell them to Mae over the radio or mount them on
  your farmhouse wall. Contracts, ranks, and six guns from Grandpa's .30-30
  to a Visitor rail gun, plus gear like heart scanners, thermal, cloak and a
  recon drone.
- Graphics presets (Low to Ultra) in Settings. Laptops: start on Low.
